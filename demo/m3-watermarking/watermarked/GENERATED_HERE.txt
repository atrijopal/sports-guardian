Watermarked copies are generated here by Module 3.

In the demo:
  1. Upload broadcast.mp4 with Viewer Name = "Alice"   → alice_watermarked.mp4
  2. Upload broadcast.mp4 with Viewer Name = "Bob"     → bob_watermarked.mp4
  3. Upload broadcast.mp4 with Viewer Name = "Charlie" → charlie_watermarked.mp4

These files are created automatically by the /api/embed endpoint.
